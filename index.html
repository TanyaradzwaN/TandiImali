About Me

Leadership measured by lives transformed.

My name is Tandi Mapolisa and I am an Entrepreneur, Community Leader, Philanthropist, and Author. I am the Founder of the Tandi Mapolisa Foundation, an organisation dedicated to bridging gaps in education, economic empowerment, health awareness, and community development across Zimbabwe.

Driven by compassion and a desire to create sustainable impact, I work to empower individuals to become self-reliant, confident, and capable of building better futures for themselves and their communities.
