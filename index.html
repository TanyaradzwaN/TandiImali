About Me

Leadership measured by lives transformed.

My name is Tandi Mapolisa, and I am an Entrepreneur, Philanthropist, and Author. I am the Founder of the Tandi Mapolisa Foundation.

Driven by compassion and a desire to create sustainable impact, I am committed to giving hope to those who may have lost it, reminding them that their circumstances do not have to define their future. I am particularly passionate about empowering women and the girl child, creating opportunities for them to discover their potential, build confidence, become self-reliant, and believe in what is possible.

Through my work, I seek to inspire individuals and communities to rise above their circumstances, create better futures, and become a source of hope for others.

Where there is hope, there is still a reason to keep going.

My Book

Full Circle – Charting My Way

What if the circumstances you were born into did not have the final say in who you would become?

Full Circle – Charting My Way is a powerful story of a young girl raised in Mbare, Harare, Zimbabwe, who became a teenage mother but refused to let her circumstances define her future. Through faith, family, determination, and resilience, she charted a path that led her to become a respected voice in the payroll and human capital industry. A story of rising beyond your past, the power of family, building people like family, and believing that your possibilities are only as limited as you allow them to be. Coming Soon.

Your beginning does not have to determine your ending.

Community Work

The Tandi Mapolisa Foundation

The Tandi Mapolisa Foundation exists to give hope where it is needed most and create opportunities where they may seem out of reach.

Through education, youth empowerment, women's development, financial literacy, and community outreach, we work alongside individuals and communities to equip them with the knowledge, confidence, and resources they need to build better lives. We believe that every person deserves an opportunity to rise, regardless of where they come from or the circumstances they face. Our work is about more than providing assistance; it is about restoring hope, creating opportunity, and empowering people to become self-reliant and shape their own futures. For our donors, partners, and supporters, every contribution is an opportunity to change a life. Together, we can turn compassion into action and create lasting impact.

One person, one family, and one community at a time.

Giving hope. Creating opportunities. Changing lives.
